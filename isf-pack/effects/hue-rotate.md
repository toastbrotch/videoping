# Hue rotate

`hue-rotate.fs` is an ISF effect from the videoping shader pack. Turns all colors around the color wheel or keeps them cycling; saturation from black and white to wildly over-saturated.

## Sliders

| Slider | Range | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hue** | 0 – 1 | 0 | Fixed turn around the color wheel. |
| **Speed** | −1 – 1 | 0 | Keeps the colors cycling; negative = the other way. |
| **Saturation** | 0 – 4 | 1 | Saturation: 0 = black and white, 1 = normal, 4 = wildly over-saturated. |

## Tips

- **Color cycle:** Speed 0.2.
- **Swapped colors:** Hue 0.5.
- **Acid:** Saturation 4, Speed 0.6.
