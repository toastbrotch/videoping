# Duotone

`duotone.fs` is an ISF effect from the videoping shader pack. Shadows in one hue, highlights in another. High contrast gives a hard two-color poster.

## Sliders

| Slider | Range | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dark hue** | 0 – 1 | 0.7 | Hue of the shadows. |
| **Light hue** | 0 – 1 | 0.12 | Hue of the highlights. |
| **Contrast** | 0.5 – 8 | 1 | Contrast between the two. Up = a hard two-color poster. |

## Tips

- **Purple and gold:** the defaults.
- **Poster:** Contrast 8.
- **Cyan/red:** Dark hue 0.5, Light hue 0.
