# Sharpen

`sharpen.fs` is an ISF effect from the videoping shader pack. Sharpens the picture (unsharp mask): a little crisps up a soft clip, a lot gives hard halos and a crunchy, over-processed look.

## Sliders

| Slider | Range | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Amount** | 0 – 20 | 0.8 | How strong: 0.8 = crisper, 20 = hard halos and crunchy noise. |
| **Radius (px)** | 0.5 – 40 | 1.5 | Size of the detail that is sharpened: small = fine detail, big = broad halos around shapes. |
| **Leave flat areas** | 0 – 0.5 | 0 | Leaves areas with little detail (noise, soft gradients) alone. |
